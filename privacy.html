Privacy

SnapPDF processes your images entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, logged or tracked. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics.

What happens to your files

When you add images, they are read by JavaScript running on your own device and assembled into a PDF on that same device. The file you download is created locally and saved by your browser. At no point does any image leave your machine — SnapPDF has no server that could receive it.

What we collect

  • No images or file contents — they never reach us.
  • No personal data — there are no accounts and no forms.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking of any kind.

Third parties

None. All code is served as static files; no third-party scripts run on the page.
